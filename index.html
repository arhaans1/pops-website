Fathers don't lack love. They lack a system.

Modern fathers are asked to do three things at once: perform at work, provide for the family, and be emotionally present at home. But no one ever gives you a system that integrates all three.

So you default to what you were taught - provide first, connect later. And "later" never comes.

  • •Physically present in the room. Mentally still in the meeting.
  • •Working hard to give your family everything - except the one thing they need most.
  • •You try. It does not stick. You feel guilty. You try again.
Fathers do not lack love. They lack a consistent way to express it. They default into disconnection - not because they do not care, but because no one ever taught them how.

That changes here.
